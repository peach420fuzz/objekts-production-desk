## Positioning judgement (inference from six providers, explicitly limited)

These offers demonstrate a real existing **previs → techvis → capture data → postvis/VFX** market. The differentiator for objekts is *not* inventing pre-shoot planning. It is offering a **narrow, paid, remote, cross-method shot decision service** that can operate before a producer commits to a full VFX vendor, plus controlled assessment of where generative methods are technically suitable and where they are not.

### Buyer-specific job statements

- **Producer/line producer:** “Which shots could force a reshoot or expensive rebuild, and what decision must be made before the shoot?”
- **Director/DP:** “How can we retain intended blocking and lens language while giving post sufficient geometry, exposure and reflections?”
- **VFX producer/supervisor:** “What did production actually agree to capture, what assumptions are testable, and what should the on-set team prioritize?”
- **Post vendor:** “Which exact files, transforms, plates, calibration and metadata will be handed over with shot IDs and versioned approvals?”

### Purchaseable deliverable, not free general advice

1. Script/boards/shot-list audit with shot IDs, creative locks and constraints.
2. **Method decision:** practical / 2D / CGI / bounded generative / hybrid, with reasons, unresolved evidence and confidence.
3. **Critical gate:** smallest pre-shoot test likely to falsify an assumption; fix or redesign before commitment.
4. **Optical/camera feasibility:** lenses, focus/zoom, gate/crop, movement, perspective, rolling shutter, exposure; distinguish a physically recoverable solve from unverified hope.
5. **Conditional crew capture request:** camera files/metadata, clean plates, lens grids, survey, reference, witness, lighting/colour captures as required by actual chosen method.
6. Cost/schedule assumptions and a versioned **handoff for the production-appointed VFX supervisor / DP / DIT / post vendor**.

Fee is agreed per production, no default public flat rate. On-set attendance, equipment, crew, physical capture, permits, safety and final post/VFX are **separate**.

### Avoid these market mistakes

- Selling “previs” while providing only static AI concept art; actual techvis needs geometry and validated spatial camera math.
- Advertising “we guarantee fewer reshoots / cheaper shots”: this scan contains no causal performance data; say “identify pre-shoot failure risks.”
- Pretending a diffusion output faithfully solves foreground occlusions, temporal continuity or reflected cinematography without tests.
- Overclaiming metadata collection: producer must arrange an appointed responsible person and capture in accordance with physical production realities.
- Claiming independent LLM endorsement because an author-submitted skill is indexed.
- Promoting cases/credits without cleared rights (current suppression in role24.1: Cordiant, Made Sense, SmartFruits as *new external promotional proofs*).
