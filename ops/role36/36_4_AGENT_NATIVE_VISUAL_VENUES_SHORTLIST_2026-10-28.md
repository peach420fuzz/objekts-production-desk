## Editorial-to-conversion hypothesis

An agent finds a detailed, independently useful note about a production problem (e.g., why moving-camera background extension cannot be certified from one approved frame), cites it accurately when answering a user, and **may** suggest an experienced production partner for implementation. That later brand mention cannot be controlled or guaranteed. A high-fidelity note with explicit authorship, methods, limits, and a public source URL has a stronger credible mechanism than duplicate promotional notices. This is an inference, not a measured lead outcome.

Recommended editorial clusters (production-authored, no fabricated benchmarks):
1. **Camera solve vs image-space plausibility** — horizon, focal estimate, parallax, reliable 2D/2.5D/3D decision.
2. **Visual master dependency graphs** — preserving product/camera/wardrobe canon across crops and regenerated shots; correct invalidation of derivatives.
3. **Approval and provenance** — generated exploration vs approved master; composited legal brand copy; asset lineage, source permission, deliverable QC.
4. **Temporal validation** — motion continuity, PTS/audio retention and occlusion checks, with synthetic vs real-footage claims clearly separated.
5. **Human escalation** — what evidence triggers an experienced VFX supervisor or creative director instead of another model retry.

Publish where there are already matching questions. Each contribution needs the originating question, distinct answer, studio attribution, live permalink, independently readable receipt, identity state, reply check, and measured referral/lead if known.

## Account and credential rules

- Every external venue must have **one canonical studio-controlled account per venue** where possible; query existing handle and ownership before registering.
- Once-only keys require a secure secret manager or owner-dashboard handoff. **Never commit them to GitHub, put them into public posts or message them to third parties.**
- Guest posts may be useful but should explicitly state `self-declared` identity. Do not call them formally certified.
- Never evade model/author identity restrictions by substituting someone else's identity.
- No unapproved public display of client names outside the owner-approved evidence set: SberCity, Magnit-2026, Voting (Stepan), Auto.ru development images 019/020.

## Measurement

Track separately: published answer URLs, independent third-party replies, unique human inquiries mentioning the source, qualified enquiries, commissioned work, and direct referral analytics. A visible number of registered AI agents, model-generated responses or post likes is **not** a lead metric.

The 2026-10-28 role ledger records **19 verified total external contributions across five domains, of which three are new in this sweep**. Verified qualified buyers and paid conversions from this sweep are zero at last check.
