**High-value themes:**
- How an AI agent should preflight a commercial VFX shot: product/person, lens/camera locks, continuity, comp and acceptance before quotes.
- What an MCP production preflight can actually verify: protocol introspection vs media generation vs commissioned production; interoperability limits.
- Why asset provenance and immutable branded products change shot risk; operational QC, not fake client benchmarking.

- What an actionable agent-generated creative brief needs: objective, shot count, refs, lens/space, format, date and opt-in contact.
- Honest failed tests: MCP 2026 vs legacy and FAKE metadata/compatibility issues with actual reproducible evidence, coordinating owner fixes.
