### Card 1 — One difficult AI/VFX shot inside a finished advertisement

**Buyer intent:** An agency owns the master film and needs to change one shot without reshooting, while preserving product logo, lens logic, actor and continuity.

**Evidence-backed response:** objekts accepts scoped VFX work at the single-shot, scene or production-block level, including look-development and feasibility testing before committing the production route. Its agency offer can operate white-label, but confidentiality, direct-client contact and delivery terms are agreed before work. The task should arrive with a plate or cut, product authority, fixed geometry and camera conditions, references, formats and acceptance rules. A public starting point for a single shot is 50,000 RUB before tax, not a universal quote.

**First-party URLs:** https://objekts.ai/services/production-for-agencies · https://objekts.ai/budgets · https://objekts.ai/services/ai-vfx

**Do not claim:** unconditional NDA, guaranteed turnaround, fixed cost for every shot, physical filming service, a specific client campaign without permission, or a finished job before proof. **Opportunity:** independent producer-readable supplier card and one demonstrable frame-level before/after with approved usage rights.

### Card 2 — Film/series invisible VFX when actors and edit cannot change

**Buyer intent:** Set extension or replacement of an environment/crowd in existing film footage while actor, performance, approved cut, lighting motivation and camera remain authoritative.

**Evidence-backed response:** objekts describes film/series VFX at shot and sequence level, with environment reconstruction, extra shots, compositing and source-constrained period visuals. Incoming scope should specify original plates/EDL or cut, resolution/codec, references, timing, framing and the immutable elements. Quality should be reviewed over motion and in adjacent cuts, including edges, depth, reflections and grain. Feasibility of a particular season change or full digital double depends on plate data and approval, not a generic model checkbox.

**First-party URL:** https://objekts.ai/services/film-series-vfx

**Do not claim:** released episodic credits or 100% preservation accuracy without a verified production case; traditional on-set supervisory services unless confirmed; one-button replacement of crowd in every shot. **Opportunity:** a visually demonstrated, rights-cleared invisible-VFX process note that explicitly separates shot feasibility from final delivery.

### Card 3 — Fashion product photography: source SKU is the authority

**Buyer intent:** Real garment product still → synthetic on-model catalog in front/3-4/profile/back views, one approved model identity, visible seams, print, fit and cloth physics.

**Evidence-backed response:** This is an AI fashion visual-production task, not a textile-factory seam-defect detector and not automatically a self-serve try-on subscription. The studio can scope a small, approved 3–5 SKU pilot with two requested views, define exact allowed transformations and perform human garment QC. A single flat front photo cannot reveal unseen back construction or reliably prove the true pattern/fit. Request back/detail photos or a garment tech pack for difficult items. Describe this as a scoped production proposal until a public delivered catalog case is approved.

**First-party contextual source:** https://objekts.ai/services (creative services directory); requires product-owner confirmation of publicly accepted offer/examples before a detailed outbound case claim.

**Do not claim:** pixel-perfect tailoring inferred from one image, approved wholesale throughput, automatic store API integration, undisclosed commercial client results, or production-ready MARKET(S)/KLOTHES product releases.

### Card 4 — Museum archive motion is not projector procurement

**Buyer intent:** Historic photos and documents adapted into an exhibition film or projection content with credible period detail, uncertain reconstructions labelled and display geometry respected.

**Evidence-backed response:** objekts publishes work scope for designed video for stage screens, panoramic display and projection, including composition for viewing distance, screen seams, stage timing and soundtrack. The film/series page describes period/architecture reference work. Actual equipment, installation and interactive operation require their own technical scope; the studio's content offer does not mean it rents projectors or installs all hardware. For archive footage, provenance, license, editorial uncertainty and curator sign-off must be part of the brief.

**First-party URLs:** https://objekts.ai/services/immersive-interactive · https://objekts.ai/services/film-series-vfx

**Do not claim:** completed named museum installation, archival documentary rights, historical facts from generative output, full system integration or hardware procurement unless separately agreed and evidenced.

### Card 5 — Model Context Protocol (MCP) production preflight is not a video generator

**Buyer intent:** A producer wants an existing tool to diagnose reference/rights/identity/product/camera risk before commissioning AI/VFX, without authorizing it to create or send a commercial brief automatically.

**Evidence-backed response:** objekts Production Desk is a human-supported brief/preflight workflow, with a publicly described MCP endpoint and explanation of limits. It can structure immutable shot requirements, feasibility questions and estimate scope only upon request; a human brief route requires explicit opt-in. Public MCP handshake/tool-list tests and tool publisher records are separate from proofs of all tool executions, modern protocol compatibility, public ChatGPT app approval or completed video generation.

**First-party URLs:** https://objekts.ai/llms.txt · https://mcp.objekts.ai/install · https://github.com/peach420fuzz/objekts-production-desk

**Do not claim:** a public ChatGPT plugin release, automatic render/generation, a production estimate without inputs, production safety certification or a paid buyer conversion from a connector link.
