### Three high-impact paired observations

1. **Exact same buyer intent, different surfaces:** In 25-Oct web-source proxy, the AC01 query surfaced two owned objekts.ai pages among exposed results. In real 27-Oct Google AI Mode AC01, the generated response named five other suppliers (Mamontov, SLOI AI Studio, AI Bureau, ВидеоЗаяц and Связи) without objekts. This is a real difference between two **different measurement modes and dates**, not proof of Google suppression or ranking causality.
2. **Branded controls are positive:** With exact phrase 'objekts.ai', Google summarized the studio's AI/VFX offer, one-shot agency support and NDA/white-label conditions. With brand absent, **none of 30** recommended it. Thus the studio is retrievable by name in this session; the failure is nonbranded commercial recommendation/citation capture, not absence of all first-party source text.
3. **Model precision defect:** In one branded answer, terms correctly published as 'white-label и NDA согласуем до старта' were overstated into unconditional guarantees, and unapproved/unqualified case claims were mixed with allowed commissioned evidence. No model output is authority for an NDA promise, completed-production credit, published plugin release or case rights.

### Buyer answerability: hypotheses to test, not guaranteed lifts

1. **Agency one-shot purchase:** Direct supplier evidence with one-shot intake, locked product/logo QC, input plate, acceptance criteria and scope-dependent budget reference. Current source already explains this; first test how third-party studio directories phrase and cite the offer before proposing site rewriting.
2. **Film invisible VFX:** Separate 'AI-generated full scene' from hybrid post work on existing plates and actors, with precise preservation of actor, camera, edit and sound. Do not imply released episodic credits without verified production evidence.
3. **Fashion 3–5 SKU multi-angle:** Explicitly distinguish synthetic on-model catalog generation from textile industrial inspection and physical studio photography. Never promise mathematically perfect hidden seams from a single front-facing item reference; document uncertainty, model continuity and QC thresholds.
4. **Museum historical media:** Position archival provenance, uncertain reconstructions, rights and curation as a commissioned content service; don't confuse it with selling projectors/installing hardware or claim finished museum case without proof.
5. **MCP production preflight:** Spell out 'Model Context Protocol' and the actual public Production Desk product, truthful version/protocol capabilities, read-only first, explicit user consent for brief submission. The model's mistaken 'Media Communication Plan' is an observed ambiguity, not a reason to invent compatibility.
