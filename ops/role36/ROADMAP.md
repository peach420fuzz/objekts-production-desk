## Living roadmap · v1.0 · 25 October 2026

**Owner:** role 36. **Goal:** make [objekts](https://objekts.ai) independently discoverable to LLMs, AI agents, producers and commissioning buyers, and turn *traceably sourced* discoveries into qualified human production briefs. **Market order:** Russia → CIS → Thailand/SEA → Europe and other international markets. **Budget:** free listings and owned infrastructure; no paid directory promotion, API subscriptions or inflated promises.

This roadmap is executed against [the public syndication ledger](../../references/EXTERNAL_SYNDICATION_LEDGER_2026-10-15.md) and [the dated discovery audit](../../references/DISCOVERY_EVIDENCE_2026-10-24.md). Reuse one canonical identity and one deduplicated contact history.

### ACTIVE RULES / immutable boundaries

1. **Brand and evidence:** Always write `objekts` in lower case. Official proof cases: SberCity and Magnit with correct production role/credits. Auto.ru is **development/test**, not a finished commissioned release; Stepan/Voting only when genuinely relevant. No fabricated awards, clients, contracts, performance or production capability.
2. **Studio service, not free rendering:** AI-native direction, AI/VFX, CGI, motion, compositing, animation and production engineering; no promise of conventional physical filming or free completed deliverables. Separate commercial studio work from agent tools' free diagnostic capability.
3. **Public status truth:** `DISCOVERED` / `SUBMITTED` / `OWNERSHIP_VERIFIED` / `PENDING_REVIEW` / `PUBLIC_VERIFIED` / `REJECTED` / `STALE`. Only `PUBLIC_VERIFIED` adds to external publication count. An HTTP 200 app shell alone does not prove a listing contains our record.
4. **Tests are not customers:** MCP `initialize` and `tools/list`, bot fetch, search impression, directory card, SEO audit, newsletter send and pending PR are distinct evidence types; none independently proves a buyer referral, qualified lead, acceptance or revenue.
5. **Canonical source:** first-party pages and rights/credits are authoritative; external copies must point back to the relevant case, product endpoint or published technical evidence. Never rehost private client assets or grant third parties unpublished footage.
6. **No duplicates, no spam:** check an exact company/contact/endpoint in the ledger, Gmail SENT and the destination before another submission. Revise copy for each editorial/technical audience. Respect provider rate limits; no alternate-account bypass.
7. **Zero discretionary spend:** refuse paid/featured/Verified upgrades by default; do not open subscription-backed tools or trigger paid rendering. For OAuth or legal publisher attestations requiring the owner, stop at an explicit handoff.
8. **Safe production writes:** diagnose website, DNS and Workers before edits; create only tightly scoped records and verify rollback. Do not redeploy the main site or override another role's live code.
9. **Private products stay private:** recording a remote MCP test does not publish an OpenAI/Claude app. FAKE web-image generation remains an unverified product gate; no App Directory re-submission before product owner's acceptance.
10. **Receipt discipline:** each outcome records UTC/Madrid date, exact domain/URL, action, independent response/ID, proof type, owner, follow-up date and ledger status. Never infer success from intent.
