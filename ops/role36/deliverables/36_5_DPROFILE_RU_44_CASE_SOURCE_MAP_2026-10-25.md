## What this actually demonstrates

- Together with [30 external editorial works](./36_5_VISUAL_SOURCE_MAP_2026-10-25.md) and [27 Behance cases](./36_5_CREATOR_NETWORK_COMPARATIVE_SCAN_2026-10-25.md), this gives **101 distinct case-page URLs** (30+27+44). Their project identities and original release dates were not independently deduplicated across venues; **101 source pages does not mean 101 newly released films**. At least two examples in this Dprofile set are older authored works uploaded in 2026. This sample is deliberately skewed toward 3 venues, motion and generative-video search terms; it cannot ground broad market-share/trend claims.
- RU creative discovery **is not only social posting**: Dprofile permits agency/studio profiles and mixed-media case narratives. The official [help](https://dprofile.ru/help) indicates 2 project slots and 4 fragment slots in a rolling 30 days on its basic account. There is no reason to pay for PRO; event/award submission is a separate potential paid activity and outside zero-budget scope.
- Highest case-study relevance: [ЛУКОЙЛ / SHAR](https://dprofile.ru/case/185292/lukoil-ai-grafika-i-mousn-dizain) shows exactly scoped AI graphics plus additional generated shots and replaced seasons; [Капральский берег](https://dprofile.ru/case/197216/ai-videorolik-dlia-zk-kapralskii-bereg) documents preserving architectural renders against unwanted alteration; [ТОК](https://dprofile.ru/case/194135/otkryvaiushhee-video-dlia-konferencii-tok-2026-tocka-bank) ties style frames to animation timing; [Ночь в Холмах](https://dprofile.ru/case/196452/noc-v-xolmax) names fourteen projectors and physical projection space; [CHA U KAO](https://dprofile.ru/case/197322/cha-u-kao-imidzevyi-3d-rolik) has unusually explicit product, materials, compositional world and pipeline. These are author-page statements, not independently verified outcomes.
- Other useful axis: [Kaspersky](https://dprofile.ru/case/192982/kaspersky-true-to-business) credits visual/production team and a branded form across environments; [KINN](https://dprofile.ru/case/193632/kinn) has an authored physical-object metaphor and clear credit split; [FACE II FACE](https://dprofile.ru/case/181213/face-ii-face) explicitly rejects AI use. Thus contemporary work in the sample includes traditional craft as well as AI; do not mistake AI prevalence in search results for all market practice.
