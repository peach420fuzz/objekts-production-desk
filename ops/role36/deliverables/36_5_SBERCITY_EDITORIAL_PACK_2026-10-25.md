### RU summary for editorial case
Сезон можно заменить на посте. Сложнее сделать так, чтобы в кадре остались прежними люди, их движения и сам город. В двух фильмах для СберСити мы работали с AI VFX, графикой и генеративными сценами: сохраняли съёмочную игру и физический контакт, а новые состояния среды собирали по слоям. Зимний квартал, снег, свет и архитектура должны были убедительно сосуществовать в одной перспективе. В работе важен не сам инструмент, а точность границ: что нельзя менять и что можно достроить. Два фильма — «Новый год» и «Времена года» — показывают разные масштабы этой задачи.

### EN short summary
Winter can be added to an image. Preserving the performance, touch and identity of a real place is the harder problem. Across two SberCity films, objekts contributed AI VFX, graphics and — for Seasons — generative scenes. Filmed performances remained an anchor while new environmental layers were integrated through perspective, light, scale, focus and atmospheric continuity. The work is less about a transformation trick than about deciding which parts of a frame must remain unchanged.

### EN editorial narrative (long)
For SberCity, the challenge was to move a recognizable urban environment through different seasonal states without displacing the people who inhabit it. The image had to keep its documentary anchors: the actors' performance, the physical contact with snow and objects, and the architecture that identifies the location.

The production method changed from scene to scene. Where gestures and weight mattered, filmed action and practical reference provided the base. AI-assisted and CG elements could then extend the space, shift the atmosphere or build specific shots. Each layer had to conform to a camera that already existed, and to the lighting and optical behaviour of the original material. A successful change of season should not silently change the geography of the scene.

objekts' documented contribution was AI VFX and graphics for New Year, and AI VFX, graphics and generative scenes for Seasons. The final work was assessed for faces, edges, scale, focus, atmosphere and grain. In these films, the most interesting visual effects are the ones that leave the human scene intact.
