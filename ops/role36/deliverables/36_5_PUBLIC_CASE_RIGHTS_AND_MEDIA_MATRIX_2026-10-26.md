## Canonical asset intake to close each row
An external Pinterest/Behance/Are.na/editorial upload requires a **specific asset record**, not a universal client release:
1. `case_url`; `asset_id`; publicly displayed preview URL; underlying **native master filepath/id** and SHA256; pixel dimensions, aspect ratio, codec/ICC; original/campaign date; exact frame and scene.
2. `rights_holder`, `production_agency`, `client`, `producer_signoff`, `client_publicity_signoff` with real evidence and scopes (site / social / editor preview / print / sublicensing / geography / term). Separate copyright in final frame from source/live-action plate, performances, voice/music and trademarks.
3. `visual_art_director`, production-team credit string approved by rights holder; no implied DP/film director credit where only localization/VFX is known.
4. `source_components` + faces/likeness/locations/logos; release risk noted, not automatically judged permissible because images are small or synthetic.
5. `approved_crop` (exact framing, framing consent), output colour/bit-depth, QR/CTA URL and UTM content ID. Maintain media truth: a preview/upscale is never relabelled original master.
6. `outbound_owner` role13 editorial email or role15 platform self-post; actual approval UTC timestamp; `PUBLIC_VERIFIED` signed-out URL; credited attribution; 7/28-day referral read by role12. UNKNOWN → **block external publication**, not silently assume.
