## 2. Are.na channels, qualitatively inspected
1. [Motion — Type](https://www.are.na/channel/motion-type-rl8fnrdlzic) by Yann Valiani, public page modified 9 days before research (as shown 25 Oct), 154 blocks; contains distinct studios and source-linked kinetic typography specimens. Lesson: one medium with a clear typographic movement criterion, not a dumping board. Do not republish other creators' GIFs as studio output.
2. [Motion: Distortion](https://www.are.na/channel/motion-distortion) by Vincent Owen, modified ~7 days before research, 69 blocks; direct written criterion for stretched/refracted/warped footage. Lesson: describe an observable physical or cinematic rule, not mood-board adjectives.
3. [motion](https://www.are.na/channel/motion-ubhayh1nm4c), 25 blocks, modified ~13 days before research; mix of animation snippets and named motion projects, source and unlinked items. Lesson: a channel needs thorough originating-artist attribution, particularly for mixed uploads.

These channels are not studio-owned and contain works spanning years. The modified date is **channel curation activity**, not each block’s date.

## 4. Three repeatable presentation mechanisms (inference, 27 cases)
**Pattern A — Editorialized technical proof.** B03/B04/B05/B07/B24 show a problem, a transformation (plate/track/objects, material light, composite), and a restrained final. For objekts: select a static SberCity frame; then material plane, spatial boundaries/occlusions, final frame. If source plates cannot be released, show only permissible generalized diagrams or skip.
**Pattern B — Honest author-credit grammar.** B01/B02/B03/B19/B25/B26 offer named role breakdowns and coauthors. For objekts: specific studio scope/agency/client roles, embedded credits; no “we created everything” when external filming and cast exist.
**Pattern C — One visual thesis, multiple media.** B08/B09/B10/B18/B25 pair single material/character/spatial thesis with storyboard/frame motion. Behance is linear narrative; Pinterest atomizes to an authored image subject; Are.na is a web of credited ideas. Derivative content should differ by editorial function, not just repeated crop.

## 5. Three artifacts to avoid
- Monotone AI service montage where every project title is a synonym for “cinematic premium generated video” (B11/B12/B17 signal the risk, not an accusation of low quality). Fix: one constrained art-direction claim per piece.
- Generic sci-fi HUD/hologram/chrome as shorthand for innovation (some B23 type). Fix: physical motivation and particular object/story stakes.
- Author ambiguity — speculative projects imply a brand commission, agency contributions missing, archive/licensed footage uncredited (B14/B18/B21 show why status labels matter). Fix: explicit spec/client/agency/licensing language.
