## B. Observed distribution patterns (hypotheses grounded in these 30 examples)
1. **The work is named before the tool.** Stash features a specific object, frame, action or cinematic problem; production methods follow. Implication: lead the SberCity public case with seasonal continuity, not “AI generative filmmaking”.
2. **Credited craft multiplies editorial credibility.** Full client, director, production, CG, sound and grade lists appear in strong Stash cases. Implication: no upload of incomplete SberCity credit field as if studio did all work.
3. **Self-initiated films can compete with commissioned work.** Kim Soobin, Pleid St., Karim Dabbèche and Studio River's explicitly labeled spec were covered. Implication: find an original rights-cleared objekts experiment to reduce client clearance burden.
4. **The film is accompanied by the question it solves.** Optical Arts has physics/choreography; Illo form semantics; FRAME tool limitation/creative control; Sostav detailed casting and finishing. Implication: publish 2–4 exact failure/repair decisions, not a collage of AI images.
5. **Not everything is screen-futuristic.** Painted frames, paper, stitching, botanical forms and analogue construction are current editorial subjects. Implication: brand-canon-led tactile cinema is a valid route to attention.

## C. Three clichés to reject
- **Platform-agnostic “AI / cinematic / cutting-edge / immersive” boilerplate**, visually unsupported.
- **Uncredited all-studio credit on a multi-team film**, especially client/agency/live-action/musician contributions.
- **Mandatory 2:3 crops and endless near-identical Pins** that destroy optics/composition. Original frame may stay widescreen or not be posted.
