## Milestone gates

- G1 S05: dated source registry, owners and explicit unknowns.
- G2 S10: sourced research map and 222 independently testable conditions.
- G3 S20: metadata, payment and friction experiments defined with controls.
- G4 S30: reproducible safe benchmark specification, not claims of live success.
- G5 S35: product packets or explicit BLOCKED state for all named products.
- G6 S40: accepted owner requests, checked deployment regressions where applicable, and honest lead/cost measurement.
