## Editorial angle (one paragraph)

AI assistants already participate in consumer product research and B2B vendor comparisons, while traditional page-click attribution records a shrinking portion of some search journeys. Creative-production services carry job-specific constraints that ordinary product catalogues rarely describe: continuity, filmed-plate rights, exact contributor credits, proof-of-concept requirements and human approval. From Social to Agentic Networks examines this discovery problem using published primary industry studies, protocol documents, a transparent eight-GitHub-organization negative-control sample and four source-qualified objekts production examples. It proposes a way to measure eligibility, proof, consent and real production inquiries without claiming a viral result.

## Shareable empirically sourced observations

1. **Pew:** Google users in a US browsing panel clicked result links on 8% of visits with AI summaries versus 15% without, observational March/April 2025 method. [Pew source](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/).
2. **6sense:** 94% of B2B buyer study participants used LLMs in their purchasing journey; they still consulted vendors. [6sense source](https://6sense.com/science-of-b2b/buyer-experience-report-2025/).
3. **objekts observation:** Of eight preselected major animation/VFX organizations' *first returned public technical repos*, six READMEs were retrievable; zero of these six were commercial production brief intake cards. This is **not** a company-level readiness ranking. [Raw dataset](../research/2026-10-29-studio-github-readme-audit.json).
4. **2026 standards:** ARD describes registered tools and skills; ACP/UCP describe retail/commerce capabilities. These do **not** imply that a bespoke production service is already eligible for a particular platform's registry. [Google ARD source](https://developers.googleblog.com/announcing-the-agentic-resource-discovery-specification/).
