# Product Fidelity Production

Use for product films, FMCG, bottles, packaging, automotive, devices, fashion objects, packshots, or any visual-production job where the hero object must remain exact.

## Diagnose before generating

Separate these questions:
- What approved source controls product geometry?
- What controls packaging and label layout?
- What controls logo and typography?
- What controls material response / reflections?
- Which camera angles are actually supported by the source assets?
- Does the approved source survive motion, parallax and close-ups?

An approved packshot can be authoritative and still be **FIT WITH REBUILD**.

## Routing rule

Use generation where variation is useful.
Use deterministic graphics / controlled CGI / source assets where exactness matters.
Do not let free generation invent unavailable product views, legal typography, logo geometry or packaging.

## Continuity PoC

When the product repeats across shots, prove three representative shots before producing the full sequence.

Success criterion:
the same approved geometry, packaging, materials and key color cues survive all representative shots without redesign between shots.
