## Production signature, grounded in the work

The studio's authorship is visible in its **choice and control of production methods**: the source plate determines the camera, a product packshot determines the actual object, practical contact constrains scene geometry, and the final composition is reviewed for light, materials, identity, depth and continuity. These decisions appear across different visual treatments; the brand does not imply one permanent colour grade or rendering recipe.

For project-specific production assistance, send references, source materials and the exact visual constraint to **salute@objekts.ai**.
