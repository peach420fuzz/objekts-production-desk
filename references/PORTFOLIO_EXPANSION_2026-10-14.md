## Additional production findings not in the original seven-case feed

### Magnit

**Evidence:** Published film and distinct development studies.
**Constraint and method:** The photographic plate owns performer motion, camera trajectory, focus and occlusion. Retail geometry and signage are held stable while missing environment and character design are developed in separate tracks.

- [Project / research source](https://objekts.ai/en/work/magnit-2026)
- [Image-led editorial production note](https://objekts-public-visual-index.peach420fuzz.workers.dev/stories/magnit-hybrid-vfx)


### Oakheart Black

**Evidence:** 127 working images, not 127 released campaign assets.
**Constraint and method:** A controlled official packshot fixes bottle geometry and typography while sets, fire, wood, orange colour and glass-light transmission vary. Defect review is a separate gate.

- [Project / research source](https://objekts.ai/en/work/oakheart-black)
- [Image-led editorial production note](https://objekts-public-visual-index.peach420fuzz.workers.dev/stories/oakheart-product-worlds)


### Navka Show — The Queen of Spades

**Evidence:** 29-second excerpt and visual development.
**Constraint and method:** 4:3 silent-cinema staging, fixed cameras, expressive but physically grounded gestures, period sets, costume continuity and shadows; not a full show recording.

- [Project / research source](https://objekts.ai/en/work/navka-show-tests)
- [Image-led editorial production note](https://objekts-public-visual-index.peach420fuzz.workers.dev/stories/navka-show-silent-cinema)


### Voting — Three Films

**Evidence:** Final films and working breakdowns.
**Constraint and method:** Scale, clothing state and props across edits; progressive psychological reaction and identity preservation; choir movement tied to a musical phrase.

- [Project / research source](https://objekts.ai/en/work/voting)
- [Image-led editorial production note](https://objekts-public-visual-index.peach420fuzz.workers.dev/stories/voting-three-films)


### Donstroy — Tatarskaya 35

**Evidence:** Visual test, not a stated finished commissioned film.
**Constraint and method:** The source photograph controls architecture, viewpoint and crop; lighting and material studies cannot bend walls or invent a new perspective.

- [Project / research source](https://objekts.ai/en/work/donstroy-tests)
- [Image-led editorial production note](https://objekts-public-visual-index.peach420fuzz.workers.dev/stories/donstroy-architectural-test)


### The Invisible Silhouette

**Evidence:** Independent visual R&D, client not established.
**Constraint and method:** Silhouette, folds and shoes make running, jumping and street movement legible without a visible person. This is separate from fashion catalogue SKU manufacturing.

- [Project / research source](https://objekts.ai/en/work/fashion-in-motion)
- [Image-led editorial production note](https://objekts-public-visual-index.peach420fuzz.workers.dev/stories/fashion-in-motion)


### KLOTHES

**Evidence:** R&D, no validated 1000-SKU throughput.
**Constraint and method:** Approved model pack, garment source authority, SKU mapping and exception handling. Identity fidelity and unseen garment construction need source references and manual QC.

- [Project / research source](https://objekts.ai/en/research/klothes-catalog-system)
- [Image-led editorial production note](https://objekts-public-visual-index.peach420fuzz.workers.dev/stories/klothes-product-fidelity)


### FAKE

**Evidence:** R&D character system.
**Constraint and method:** Facial geometry, hair, skin and age cues belong to an approved character state. Repeatability requires locks, versioning and controlled variations.

- [Project / research source](https://objekts.ai/en/research/fake-character-system)
- [Image-led editorial production note](https://objekts-public-visual-index.peach420fuzz.workers.dev/stories/fake-character-system)
