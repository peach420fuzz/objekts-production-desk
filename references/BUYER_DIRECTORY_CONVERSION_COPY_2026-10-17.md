## Conversion goal

A directory listing should do five things before asking anyone to contact us:

1. Explain the production *problem we solve*, not generic “AI innovation.”
2. Explain the smallest practical unit of work (a defined shot) and when we can own a complete commercial.
3. Show a **verifiable source case and precise credit** for each capability.
4. Describe the producer's minimum input, review gates, and deliverables.
5. Invite the producer to send a specific problem to **salute@objekts.ai**. No speculative “unlimited content”, invented deadlines, or unverified performance claims.

**Headline / tagline (83 characters):**
Director-led AI/VFX, CGI & cinematic commercial production — scoped to your brief

**Short description (for search previews):**

objekts is a boutique visual-production studio for commercial directors, agencies and production partners. We deliver difficult AI/VFX shots, coherent short-form advertising films, product CGI and art-directed visual development. Every job starts with source authority, camera and product continuity, and a clear review scope. Barcelona-based, working remotely on international briefs.

**Full profile overview:**

objekts turns visually difficult advertising ideas into controllable production tasks. We can join an existing shoot to finish one precise shot, take responsibility for a defined AI/VFX sequence, or deliver a complete short-form commercial. Our work spans CGI, generative image and motion tools, art direction, post-production and compositing. The tool is chosen after reviewing the visual constraints.

Three practical ways to work:

- **Finish a shot.** Start with the approved plate. Protect camera trajectory, optics, actor timing, scale, masking, occlusion and contact shadows. Accept or reject against the brief rather than a single hero still.
- **Build a commercial.** Set the visual language, approved master references, shot list and revision gates before scaling generation, CG and finishing. Deliver a sequence with consistent edit, continuity and brand legibility.
- **Develop a visual world.** Create controlled product, environment or character variants while locking bottle geometry, packaging, materials, identity and mandatory views. Research is separated from released client work.

Proof: [SberCity](https://objekts.ai/en/work/sbercity) (AI VFX, graphics and selected AI scenes), [Tic Tac](https://objekts.ai/en/work/tic-tac) (product VFX for Ballpark Production), [SmartFruits](https://objekts.ai/en/work/smartfruits) (generative production / master for BeCreative), and [Cordiant Winter](https://objekts.ai/en/work/cordiant-winter) (adaptation and product replacement for BeCreative, not origination of the full campaign).

**Next step:** send the shot/film brief, length, delivery formats, source assets, must-not-change elements, expected review timing and intended usage. We scope schedule, cost, revisions and delivery on a project-by-project basis. Confidential sources can follow an NDA and agreed secure transfer.
