## Distribution rules

1. Always spell the studio **objekts**. Never claim other people's similarly named moodboards.
2. Every source page includes its production scope, case link, and whether it is released advertising or R&D. Do not convert 127 Oakheart studies into 127 commissioned ads.
3. Media hotlinks on the studio website do not automatically clear third-party binary reuploads. Rights remain unverified until confirmed by the owner.
4. No paid placements, rate-limit workarounds, unsolicited bulk replies, fabricated profiles, or duplicate directory registrations.
5. Update this ledger with the **destination URL and acceptance evidence**, or record a named blocker. Keep RSS Amplifier's five feeds as one distribution platform, not five companies.
6. To broaden the audience, prioritize new *external* platforms that accept studios and artistic production work, not additional near-duplicate subdomains or merely preparatory CSV files.
