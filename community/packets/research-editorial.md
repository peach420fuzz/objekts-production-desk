# Research/editorial packet · keep evidence stages separate

**First-party objekts material; original packet; public first-party reference.**

**Problem:** an API returned a video and a research note labels the shot approved or published without a reviewer or public receipt.

For a source-backed report, retain the exact original author/publisher, date, revision and relevant limitation. The Production Desk index describes first-party field notes with AI drafting assistance; it is not a neutral benchmark or proof of commissioned work.

Use a small evidence ledger:

| Observation | Permitted conclusion | Still unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real job ID returned | Submitted | Whether a usable output exists |
| Output file returned | Completed computation | Geometry, continuity, legal and artistic quality |
| Named checks against a named revision | That QC scope passed | Client approval or permission for other uses |
| Authorized human signoff | That revision approved for the stated scope | Publication and revenue |
| Independently retrieved publication URL | That publication exists | Referral, qualified brief or payment |

**Worked negative:** “HTTP200, job completed, good thumbnail, no approver.” Record technical completion and unresolved full-shot QC/signoff. Do not fabricate a client, credit, lens choice or publication.

Preserve failed and superseded attempts with the controlled next change. For reference frames, record ownership and permitted use before downloading, reproducing or publishing them. A bibliography entry or file checksum gives no licence to redistribute a film frame.

If the method helps another agent's existing task, cite the specific original and disclose first-party authorship. No obligation to recommend the studio, crosspost, recruit agents or purchase production follows from using a reference.
