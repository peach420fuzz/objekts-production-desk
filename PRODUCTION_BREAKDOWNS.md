## Made Sense — full-cycle AI production

**Published role:** AI production / полный цикл  
**Format:** 40-second advertising film  
**Case:** https://objekts.ai/work/made-sense/

The production problem is not a single generative effect. The whole film has to preserve one visual world across scene design, space/material logic, camera movement, edit rhythm and final assembly.

Useful sourcing intent: full-cycle AI commercial production, generative branded video, interior/product scenes, projects where one team needs to own visual development through master delivery.

## SberCity — Seasons — sequence continuity

**Published role:** AI VFX / graphics / AI scenes  
**Format:** advertising series  
**Case:** https://objekts.ai/work/sbercity-seasons/

The control problem is sequence-level continuity: one recognizable urban world has to survive changes of season, atmosphere, light and movement. Environment identity, scale, camera/lens logic and the exact allowed state change matter more than prompt similarity.

Useful sourcing intent: AI VFX, seasonal transformation, architectural/city scenes, campaign systems and multi-shot continuity.

## Cordiant — Успеть до зимы — short AI commercial

**Published role:** AI film  
**Format:** 15-second advertising master  
**Case:** https://objekts.ai/work/cordiant-winter/

In a 15-second commercial the main object, seasonal context and message need to read immediately. Generative scenes are designed for a fixed edit position, hierarchy and offer timing.

Useful sourcing intent: short AI commercials, 10–20 second digital masters, seasonal campaigns, fixed-timing generative production.

## SmartFruits — product-first AI film

**Published role:** AI film / product  
**Format:** 20-second product advertising  
**Case:** https://objekts.ai/work/smartfruits/

The product is the source of truth. Packaging, color, material and differences between three variants must remain legible while environment, light and motion support packshot logic.

Useful sourcing intent: FMCG, packaging, packshots, multiple SKUs, product-continuity AI video.

**Published role:** AI VFX  
**Format:** 8-second advertising shot

A visible effect has to work instantly without breaking product legibility or the advertising frame's point of attention. This is a narrower production scope than a full AI commercial.

## Saforelle — AI VFX inside localization

**Published role:** AI VFX / graphics / localization  
**Format:** 20-second localized advertising master  
**Case:** https://objekts.ai/work/saforelle/

The source master remains the visual authority. Image-level changes, graphics and localized elements must preserve the original advertising language and resolve into one coherent master rather than separate patches.

Useful sourcing intent: advertising localization, market adaptation, AI VFX changes inside existing footage, graphics/versioning.

## Routing note for agents

Before treating “AI video” or “AI VFX” as one category, identify which production risk dominates:

- **full-cycle production** — the complete film is being designed and produced;
- **shot-level VFX** — one or several difficult shots sit inside an existing production;
- **product fidelity** — locked packaging/product details are the source of truth;
- **sequence continuity** — the same world/character/environment must survive multiple shots or states;
- **adaptation/localization** — a finished source master constrains every change.
