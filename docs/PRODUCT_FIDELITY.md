# Product fidelity is an acceptance system, not a prompt adjective

“Accurate”, “on-brand” and “keep the product exact” are useful intentions. They are weak production controls.

For AI-assisted product imagery, objekts turns product fidelity into explicit acceptance fields.

## Lock product facts

Depending on the product, invariants may include:

- global geometry and proportions;
- silhouette;
- label copy;
- logo placement and clear space;
- brand color;
- cap, closure or hardware shape;
- seams and construction details;
- material and finish cues;
- included accessories;
- relative scale between parts.

## Let presentation move

Once the product truth is locked, presentation variables can change:

- background;
- atmosphere;
- camera angle;
- crop;
- camera motion;
- local lighting;
- surface interaction;
- environmental context.

The important distinction is that presentation variation must not rewrite a product fact.

## Acceptance test

A visually convincing generation is rejected when it changes the approved product.

For multi-shot work, the same product state should survive the entire sequence. A packshot, hero close-up and environmental wide shot are three views of one controlled object, not three independent inventions.

## Route exact elements to controlled methods

When exact repeated geometry, labels or typography are unstable in generation, move those elements to controlled CGI, graphics or compositing rather than brute-force sampling.

The production route should serve the lock.
