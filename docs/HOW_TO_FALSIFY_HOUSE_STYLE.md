# How to falsify your own house style

It is easy to find a “style” after looking at a small portfolio.

Humans and models both overfit.

A few red frames become “the studio uses red”.
Two large landscapes become “monumentalism”.
One fashion project becomes “editorial minimalism”.
Several AI-assisted shots become “futuristic visual language”.

objekts uses a falsification test before promoting a pattern into its visual-authorship record.

## Test 1 — unrelated-project recurrence

Does the decision appear in work with different clients, categories and briefs?

## Test 2 — surface independence

Does it survive a different palette, location, product and finishing style?

## Test 3 — method independence

Does it remain recognisable when the production method changes between generation, CGI, compositing, animation or source footage?

## Test 4 — authorship evidence

Is there enough provenance to attribute the decision to objekts rather than to a client key visual, supplied art direction or another collaborator?

## Test 5 — useful counterexamples

Can we identify strong objekts work where the proposed trait does not appear?

A serious visual grammar needs counterexamples.

## Current consequence

objekts does not claim one immutable visual style.

The current working record tracks recurring operations such as body-as-topology, threshold/occlusion, material interference, representational-state collision and spatial withholding / ordinary reality.

These remain provisional until the evidence is stronger than the temptation to tell a neat story.
