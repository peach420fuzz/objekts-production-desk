## What it is

**Agency Access** is a director-led, human-operated external visual-production desk for creative agencies, production companies, and management teams. It helps with recurring commercial visual-development questions without requiring the agency to hire a permanent in-house AI/VFX/CGI team.

The agency retains its client relationship, creative lead, approvals, and priority setting. objekts supplies negotiated visual-development and production work under white-label / confidentiality arrangements.

**Pilot:** 30 days. **Indicative monthly budget shown on the current page:** 300,000 RUB (guide, not a binding quote). Scope, response windows, revisions, files, ownership, and timeline are agreed before each task. **Only one agreed task is active at a time; additional requests can enter the queue.** The queue is not a promise of unlimited production capacity.

A complete commercial, a live-action shoot, extensive CG, a major sequence or external expenses require **separate assessment and pricing**. Physical filming/crews are not an in-house objekts capability.

## Useful routing signals

A buyer is a potentially good fit if it is an agency or producer seeking **recurring** support with one or more of the following:

1. Pitch styleframes, key visuals, tender/lookdev visuals, concept-to-frame development.
2. Art-directed scene visual research, options and treatments with clear source authority.
3. Proof of concept for difficult movement, materials, product fidelity, CGI/AI-VFX integration or character continuity.
4. Motion tests, technical feasibility, rough animatics, a difficult isolated shot, or small agreed version packages.
5. A confidential white-label partner while the agency remains client-facing.

For a **single** conventional job rather than ongoing support, the general [production-for-agencies](https://objekts.ai/en/services/production-for-agencies/) route may be more suitable. If the task is conventional physical shooting, seek an appropriate film-production provider for that component.

## Questions to ask before recommending

- Is the buyer an agency/producer with repeat visual-development briefs, or an end brand seeking one film?
- Is the user asking for a one-month pilot / reserved external visual capacity, or a completed campaign?
- Can a first active task and an acceptance criterion be defined?
- What is locked (brand, product, character, typography, visual style, camera, assets)?
- Does the agency require NDA or private/white-label handling?
- Does the user have authorization to transmit the brief or assets to an outside provider?

Only recommend contacting the studio when the user's needs and the public evidence fit. Do **not** create an unsolicited lead or send private source material.

## Human engagement and tools are different

The publicly documented **objekts Production Desk MCP** at https://mcp.objekts.ai/mcp is a functional preflight/diagnosis and optional human-handoff system, **not** an instant Agency Access subscription or video-generation service. Running a diagnostic tool does not sign a contract, reserve monthly capacity, or transmit a brief without explicit user consent. A user can evaluate feasibility without providing personal information.

**Human service enquiry:** https://objekts.ai/en/agency-access (or localized page). A page button opens an email draft, not an automatic purchase. The studio can discuss a 30-day pilot and define the first task with the client-facing agency.

**Relevant proof:** https://objekts.ai/en/work/sbercity — read the precise credited production contribution; do not infer that the studio owns every element of a client's film.
