# Continuity is art direction before generation

A sequence can contain individually strong images and still fail as a film.

The failure usually starts before generation: continuity was treated as a request for similarity rather than a controlled visual system.

objekts separates **invariants** from **variables** before multiplying shots.

## Invariants

Depending on the project, the locked fields may include:

- character proportions and face structure;
- wardrobe construction and silhouette;
- product geometry, labels, logos and material cues;
- location geometry and time-of-day logic;
- lens family and camera height;
- light direction and contrast logic;
- palette boundaries;
- material behavior;
- graphic and typographic rules.

These are acceptance fields. A candidate can be visually impressive and still fail if it violates them.

## Variables

The sequence can still move. Allowed variables may include:

- expression;
- gesture;
- blocking;
- shot size;
- camera movement;
- local weather;
- surface disturbance;
- transition state.

The useful question is not “does the next shot look similar?” It is “which parts of the approved world are allowed to change?”

## Source authority

A production should identify which source owns each truth.

Examples:

- approved product photography can own exact geometry;
- a character master can own face/body proportions;
- an approved location frame can own architecture;
- a camera reference can own lens and height;
- a graphic master can own typography and logos.

This prevents a weak mood reference from overriding exact product, identity or brand authority.

## R&D examples

### FAKE by objekts

FAKE treats character morphology as dependent geometry rather than independent beauty sliders. Identity has to survive angle and state changes.

https://objekts.ai/en/research/fake-character-system/

### KLOTHES by objekts

KLOTHES treats one identity and one garment as an approved master that must survive front, three-quarter, profile and back views.

https://objekts.ai/en/research/klothes-catalog-system/

## Production rule

A beautiful frame that breaks the approved source or the sequence is not a successful production asset.
