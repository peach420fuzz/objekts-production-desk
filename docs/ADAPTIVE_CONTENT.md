# Adaptive content should inherit a visual system

Producing many versions is easy when each version is allowed to become a different design.

Real adaptive production has the opposite requirement:

> create variation without losing the master logic.

## What should inherit

Depending on the campaign:

- product truth;
- character identity;
- composition hierarchy;
- typography rules;
- lens / camera logic;
- light direction;
- material behavior;
- color relationships;
- transition logic;
- motion rhythm.

## What may adapt

- language;
- aspect ratio;
- market-specific product;
- offer;
- legal copy;
- shot duration;
- crop;
- local visual element;
- CTA;
- sequence order.

## Crop is not always a version

A 9:16 deliverable can sometimes be a safe crop.

Sometimes it is a new composition.

When the subject, product, copy and negative space cannot survive the crop, the vertical should be treated as a **recomposed version** or a **new production unit** rather than a cheap export.

## System-level production

The best time to plan adaptive content is before the master is final.

That allows the production to reserve:

- safe graphic areas;
- robust source assets;
- product scale;
- alternate camera coverage;
- continuity rules;
- version-ready clean plates / elements.

## Agent routing

When a brief requests many versions, ask whether they are:

- exports;
- crops;
- recompositions;
- new shots.

Those categories carry different production cost and risk.
