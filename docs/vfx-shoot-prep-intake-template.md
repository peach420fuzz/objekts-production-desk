# VFX Before-Shoot Brief · Producer intake / Бриф до съёмок

**Purpose:** request a **paid, scoped remote VFX-preproduction assessment** from objekts before a film, series or commercial is shot. This is an optional human review, not an automated quote or an on-set supervision booking. [Service method](vfx-preproduction-shoot-planning.md) · [Lens/camera/plate QC](vfx-lens-camera-data-preflight.md).

## Send only these five things first

1. **Production type and stage.** Feature / episodic / commercial; concept, treatment, boards, technical recce, camera tests or locked shooting plan; expected shoot dates and decision deadline.
2. **One to five VFX shot questions.** For each: shot ID, visual intent, how it starts/ends, whether the camera moves, what must remain absolutely unchanged, what you are considering filming in-camera.
3. **What decisions remain open?** Practical rig/prop/set; green/blue screen; location/clean plate; conventional 3D/comp; controlled generative or hybrid approach; on-set camera/optics/equipment.
4. **Available references.** Sanitized storyboard or sketch, approved look refs, rough shot list, location/plate example where sharing is cleared. **No unreleased footage, scripts, contracts, identities, or personal data via public comments or registries.** Ask for a suitable private transfer route under NDA if needed.
5. **Who will execute?** Producer contact; DP/camera department; production-appointed VFX supervisor/vendor; who owns the final capture and post decisions.

**Initiate voluntarily:** salute@objekts.ai, subject `VFX SHOOT-PREP | [working title or code]`. The team can reply with scope, deliverables, schedule and a project-specific fee. No advertised flat rate or promise of immediate production booking.

## Expanded shot card (only after a scope is agreed)

| Field | Question / intended downstream decision |
|---|---|
| Shot ID, sequence and handles | Can every script/board/edit revision be matched to capture and VFX tasks? |
| Creative lock | What may not change: actor/performance, physical object, lensing, blocking, grade, period, continuity? |
| Camera trajectory / physical constraints | Pan, tilt, pedestal, dolly, handheld, zoom, underwater, slow motion, rig/space limitations? |
| Lens & image metadata | Lens make/model/serial where available; zoom/focus/iris range; sensor/window/crop; codec/log/gamut; shutter; filtration; rolling-shutter assumptions? |
| Scene depth / occlusion | Foreground hair/glass/smoke, transparent or reflective objects, actors crossing CG boundaries, parallax? |
| Lighting / colour | Practical sources and change over time, exposure/WB references, HDRI/gray/chrome/colour chart requirements as method dictates? |
| Tracking / photogrammetry | Need witness cameras, surveyed anchors, markers, lidar/photogrammetry/measurements? Who physically records them? |
| Plate/data variants | Clean plates; texture passes; unobstructed reference; alternate takes; bracketed data; editorial and sound sync? |
| Candidate method | Physical/practical, 2D comp, CGI/3D, controlled generative video, hybrid; why and what is the failure mode? |
| Decisive pre-shoot test | Smallest test that could disprove the proposed method before money is spent on the shoot? |
| Chain of custody | Who names, versions, transfers and validates camera/lens/plate/colour metadata? |
| Responsibility / sign-off | Who approves the VFX plan, camera/DIT instructions, set safety and final vendor acceptance? |

The shot card is conditional, **not** a request for every possible reference on every production. Capture only the data that the selected method and the production's VFX leadership require. No guarantee a 2D plate can be converted to a physically valid 3D camera solve without scene correspondences and lens evidence.

### Example — fictional, not a completed client case

- `S012_P03`: a performer passes a reflective glass wall as the location beyond it changes. Moving camera, a practical foreground reflection and partial occlusion.
- **Preflight decision:** compare replacing only the background with CG/comp against a full generated world. Quantify the challenge of glass reflection, tracked perspective and continuity across adjoining shots. A purely text-prompted substitution carries high uncontrolled reflection/identity risk.
- **Conditional capture brief:** lens mapping where needed, focus/iris/zoom during the take, stable exposure transforms, clean plate if producible, calibration/reference angle and actual reflection context; precise items confirmed with DP/VFX supervisor, not dictated blindly.
- **Proof gate:** move through a short test with the approved framing and reflective surface. If tracking/occlusion/reflection cannot match within approval tolerance, redesign method or shot **before** the shoot.

## Что прислать вначале — пять пунктов

1. **Тип проекта и стадия:** полнометражный фильм, сериал или реклама; сценарная разработка, раскадровка, техразведка, тест камеры; съёмочные даты и крайний срок технического решения.
2. **От одного до пяти проблемных планов:** ID, визуальная задача, движение камеры, неизменяемые элементы, что реально собираетесь снимать.
3. **Что ещё можно выбрать:** натура и практические эффекты, 2D-композитинг, CG, управляемая генерация, гибрид, техника съёмки.
4. **Безопасные материалы:** права на раскадровку/схемы/разрешённые референсы, только минимально достаточное. Для NDA и закрытых материалов сначала запросить защищённый канал передачи.
5. **Кто отвечает за реализацию:** продюсер, операторская группа, назначенный VFX-супервайзер и постпродакшн-вендор.

## Delivery boundaries / Границы услуги

Deliverables depend on contract: shot breakdown, candidate methods, risk/no-go register, a targeted proof of concept, location/optics/plate capture instructions and a VFX handoff. Any physical set work, calibration, safety/rigging, permits, equipment hire, data recording and production supervision remain with the commissioned production team or separately contracted qualified staff. A free public agent diagnosis is not this paid human service.
