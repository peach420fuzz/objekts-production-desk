# SmartFruits: product first, generative environment second

**Published objekts role:** AI film / product  
**Format:** product advertising (2025)  
**Canonical case:** https://objekts.ai/work/smartfruits/

This is a first-party production note from objekts.

Product imagery has a simple hierarchy:

> the environment is allowed to become interesting only if the product remains trustworthy.

## Production problem

Generative systems are good at making product-like objects.

Advertising needs the **approved product**.

The failure modes are small but commercially important:

- changed silhouette;
- shifted proportions;
- rewritten labels;
- drifting color;
- altered cap / seam / closure;
- invented surface detail;
- inconsistent SKU differences.

## Source of truth

Product geometry and visual identifiers are treated as authority.

The environment, background, atmosphere and motion are subordinate variables.

This reverses a common generative workflow where the whole frame is sampled freely and the product is “fixed later”.

## Multiple SKUs

When several package variants appear in one film, their differences have to remain legible while the overall production system stays coherent.

The job is not to create three unrelated beautiful packages.

It is to maintain:

- shared brand family;
- controlled SKU differences;
- consistent scale;
- consistent product behavior;
- one visual logic across the sequence.

## Acceptance rule

A frame can be visually stronger and still be rejected if it changes a product fact.
