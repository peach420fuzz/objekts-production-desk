## Exact eligible-post checklist

For each proposed external post, complete every field before submit:

1. `parent_article_id`, exact source link and source version; **native destination and account/organization** identified.
2. Reader value in one sentence independent of studio discovery or clicks.
3. Clearly scoped technical factual claims with primary references, tested examples flagged, unexecuted examples marked illustrative; **human technical reviewer name/date** or HOLD.
4. Rights approval for words, screenshots, photos, diagrams, client names and performer likenesses; **reviewer name/date** or HOLD.
5. Native prose original to that audience, no unrelated keyword stuffing, affiliate agenda, fictitious testimonials or brand endorsement.
6. For DEV: explicitly mark AI assistance (e.g. `#ABotWroteThis` or in article), avoid business promotion and SEO motives; if publication objective is sales/backlinks → **do not submit to DEV**.
7. For Medium: straightforward AI assistance disclosure within first two paragraphs; no Partner Program paywall for mostly AI-generated content; substantial human editorial intervention and author responsibility.
8. Editorial byline must accurately describe the real responsible publisher. Never credit a named human writer without their approval or imply they manually wrote the complete text.
9. Real canonical URL if one exists. Do not fabricate `objekts.ai/journal/...` routes. A canonical is an attribution hint, not an eligibility or ranking guarantee.
10. After submission check a **signed-out live permalink**, timestamp, byline, disclosures, outbound links, media provenance, and rules compliance. Only then record `PUBLIC_VERIFIED`. Track referral/click/qualified brief separately.
