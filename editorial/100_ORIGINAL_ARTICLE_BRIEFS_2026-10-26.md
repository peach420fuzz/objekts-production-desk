## Quality standard for conversion from brief → public article

A brief becomes `PUBLISHED` only when it contains:
1. A real problem and diagnosis that someone could search for.
2. A reproducible demonstration: original diagram, safe fictional case, table, code or QC checklist.
3. Boundary conditions and current source verification where vendor APIs/terms are involved.
4. Explicit failure modes and evidence status; no fabricated metrics.
5. Owner/editorial rights approval if images, actual client names or performer likenesses occur.
6. A canonical source and unique social/curator adaptation. Translation is disclosed as translation, **not** counted as original new article.
7. A public signed-out URL and post date, with referrer/LLM/lead observation logged separately.

**Not a distribution instruction to mass-copy 100× across 1000 sites:** Google Search Central identifies scaled unoriginal content intended to manipulate rankings as spam: [official policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies). Build a dense network of high-quality linked ORIGINAL material and a limited number of genuinely adapted posts on suitable platforms.

**Sources are internal studio files**. The source identifiers in headings are only research provenance; none of the underlying protected project documents is uploaded to this public repository.
