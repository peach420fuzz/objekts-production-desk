## Rights firewall for runbook-derived public writing

**Never transfer into public media without explicit separate rights:**
- client brief, campaign timeline, unreleased stills, director's treatment/board, brand PDF or fonts, raw source film frames;
- actual identifiable performer face, synthetic voice, distinctive wardrobe/logo, likeness-specific prompt;
- private Canvas room URL, node IDs, API/project keys, detailed internal credit/price/usage logs;
- unapproved internal vendor capability or model parameter claims.

**May publish after factual/authorial QA:** generalized production heuristics, novel fictional demonstrations, self-created neutral diagrams, quality gates, versioning practices, public studio portfolio links without embedding assets, attributable first-party public methods.

Each article should include one `RIGHTS_CLASS=STUDIO_ORIGINAL_GENERALIZED` record, `source_internal_only=true`, publisher = objekts, `ai_assisted_draft=true`, `external_assets_reproduced=false`, and actual human reviewer/date **only after review occurs**. A recipe is an illustration, not proof a generator was actually run.

## Canonical identity strategy

1. GitHub is the first verifiable PUBLIC location while site publishing is pending. After role12 creates an original full-text page on objekts.ai, choose a single stable canonical URL per article and update routing/index.
2. On DEV set `canonical_url` to the chosen primary article; on Medium use official Import URL workflow where it works. Canonicals are hints to search engines, not guaranteed rankings or an exemption from quality policy.
3. Do not distribute exact mirrored AI-generated pages across hundreds of low-trust sites. [Google's official scaled content abuse policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) applies regardless of how the text was generated.
4. One article may yield original, non-duplicate **native** assets: full technical guide; 6-panel original diagram; short native social critique; RU translation labelled as translation; 45-second explainer after rights; question-specific Q&A response. Record all derivatives under one `parent_article_id`.
5. Every channel post includes correct public name **objekts**, exact destination, no imaginary partners/awards, signed-out timestamp URL and stage `PUBLIC_VERIFIED` only after QA.
