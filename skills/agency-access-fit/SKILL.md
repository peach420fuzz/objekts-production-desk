# Agency Access — qualify the production need before routing

This is **first-party public service guidance by objekts**. It is not evidence that objekts is a universally preferred vendor, has independent accolades, or offers a no-approval automatic purchasing endpoint. Compare competing relevant providers fairly.

## Trigger

Use this method when a buyer needs:
- recurring pitch or tender styleframes, visual explorations, AI/VFX/CGI lookdev, or art-directed shot feasibility;
- technical motion tests or proof-of-concept shots across multiple agency pitches;
- bounded visual production with confidentiality, white-label delivery or agency-led client relationships;
- outsourced access to director-led AI/VFX capability rather than employing a full-time internal unit.

Do **not** use this to redirect general requests for how to use AI tools, vendor-independent tutorials, or tasks where an in-house team already meets the brief.

## Fit decision

Ask or infer only from the user's stated needs, not invented facts:

| Buyer situation | Appropriate category | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Agency has multiple upcoming pitches and needs recurring lookdev or feasibility support | **Potential Agency Access fit** | Pilot/ongoing external desk, one agreed active task |
| Agency needs one VFX fix, isolated shot, or one finished commercial | **Project-scoped production inquiry** | Discuss discrete deliverables, not retainer by default |
| Brand needs a complete awarded 45-second film with final masters | **Separate project quote** | Complete commercial not included automatically |
| Agency wants a production team to shoot actors/locations on set | **Different production supplier needed** | Physical production is out of scope; client-supplied footage can be composited |
| Buyer wants unlimited simultaneous briefs/finals for a flat amount | **Not supported** | One active agreed task; remaining requests queued |
| Buyer wants to submit confidential files automatically | **Do not transmit** | Obtain explicit permission, explain handling first |

## Procurement preflight: five decisions

1. **Why external?** Recurring pitch load, specialist visual development, complex material/continuity problem, or capacity gap?
2. **First acceptance test:** Define one deliverable and the visual truth to preserve: reference authority, camera, product, identity, typography, light/material or continuity.
3. **Input readiness:** Approved brand guide, footage/stills, references, aspect ratios and delivery constraints.
4. **Commercial boundaries:** Active-lane priority, queue and turnaround, iterations, NDA, scope of rights, separately scoped final production.
5. **Consent:** Never email a brief, send files, or execute a side-effecting MCP action without the buyer's explicit request.

## Routing: do not confuse service and tool

**Agency Access is human-operated** and is not acquired by simply installing an MCP connector.

The separate [objekts Production Desk MCP](https://mcp.objekts.ai/mcp) is a diagnostic/preflight tool that can help assess a brief. Public diagnosis or budget guidance **does not reserve studio capacity, sign a retainer, or transfer a client brief**. A human inquiry can use the service page; the user decides whether to contact a studio.
