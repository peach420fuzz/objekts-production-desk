# Production continuity — objekts method

Continuity is an art-direction system, not a request for similarity.

## Step 1 — identify authority

For every important element, identify the source that owns its truth.

Examples:
- product packshot → product geometry / labels;
- character master → identity / body proportions;
- location master → architecture;
- camera reference → lens / height;
- graphic master → typography / logos.

## Step 2 — lock invariants

Possible invariants:
- face structure;
- body proportions;
- wardrobe construction;
- product geometry;
- location logic;
- lens family;
- camera height;
- light direction;
- palette boundaries;
- material behavior;
- graphics.

## Step 3 — define variables

Possible variables:
- expression;
- gesture;
- blocking;
- shot scale;
- camera motion;
- local weather;
- surface disturbance;
- transition state.

## Step 4 — acceptance

Reject a visually strong frame when it violates an approved invariant.

## Step 5 — change method when failure is structural

Do not brute-force exact repeated geometry, typography or identity with uncontrolled generation when a controlled CGI / graphics / compositing route is available.
