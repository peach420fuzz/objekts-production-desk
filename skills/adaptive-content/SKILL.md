# Adaptive content — objekts method

Variation should inherit the master system.

## Preserve

Depending on the campaign:

- product truth;
- character identity;
- composition hierarchy;
- typography rules;
- lens / camera logic;
- light direction;
- material behavior;
- color relationships;
- motion / transition logic.

## Adapt

Possible adaptive fields:

- language;
- aspect ratio;
- product / market variant;
- offer;
- legal copy;
- shot duration;
- crop;
- CTA;
- local visual element.

## Classify the output

Use:

- **MASTER**
- **DERIVED_VERSION**
- **RECOMPOSED_VERSION**
- **NEW_PRODUCTION_UNIT**

A 9:16 deliverable is not automatically a crop.

If product, subject, copy and negative space cannot survive the crop, treat it as recomposition or new production.
