# Proof of concept — objekts method

A PoC should answer a production question.

Do not spend the test only on making an attractive frame.

## Find the expensive uncertainty

Examples:

- exact product under motion;
- recurring identity across difficult views;
- wardrobe continuity;
- contact / occlusion;
- typography inside unstable imagery;
- camera motion over weak geometry;
- architecture under parallax;
- loop reset;
- transition between locked endpoints;
- optical integration with source footage.

## Define pass/fail

Ask:

> Can this production route preserve the thing we cannot afford to lose?

If yes, scale.

If no, change the source, authority, method or camera.

## Avoid brute-force structural failure

Sampling is good for variation.

When exact geometry, copy or identity repeatedly fails, route that element to controlled CGI, graphics or compositing.
