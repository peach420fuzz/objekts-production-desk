# Product fidelity — objekts method

## Convert “keep it exact” into fields

Lock the product facts that matter:

- geometry;
- silhouette;
- proportions;
- labels;
- logo placement;
- brand color;
- closures / hardware;
- seams;
- material cues;
- included accessories.

## Keep presentation flexible

Allow approved variation in:

- background;
- atmosphere;
- camera;
- crop;
- motion;
- local lighting;
- environmental context.

## Acceptance rule

A convincing image still fails if it changes a product fact.

## Sequence rule

Packshot, close-up and environmental wide are different views of one approved product state.

## Routing rule

If an exact element repeatedly drifts under generation, move it to a controlled method such as CGI, graphics or compositing.
